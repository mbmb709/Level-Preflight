# Level Preflight

Level Preflight gives creators a quick, local review before they share a
Geometry Dash level.

Open one of your created levels and press the small clipboard button in the
actions menu. The report checks information already stored in the level:

- whether the level has a non-default name;
- whether level content is present;
- whether a complete verification run is recorded;
- whether a description is present;
- whether user coins are verified;
- which audio and visibility options are selected;
- whether a Low Detail Mode version is marked.

The report also includes a short manual checklist for details that software
cannot judge reliably, such as gameplay readability, proper credits, device
performance, and upload settings.

Level Preflight never uploads data, contacts a server, changes a level, or
blocks the normal upload flow. Its report is guidance, not a promise that a
level will be rated or accepted.
